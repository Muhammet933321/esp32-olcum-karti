This page answers "what, how precisely, and how often".

Voltage

There are two ranges and the board switches between them automatically. Both are bidirectional — it reads negative voltage too.

⚠ 615 V is lethal. The high range is for mains and above. It connects through a separate, marked input, and while that input is in use nobody touches the board.

Power and energy

Power±5.8 kW voltage × current, signed (negative = feeding back)
Energy±2 562 Wh at 25 W it doesn't overflow for 102 hours
Charge (battery test)±2 562 Ah counts down in the charging direction
Power measurement is valid in the 40–70 Hz band. The board is calibrated at mains frequency (50/60 Hz) and the calibration no longer holds outside that band. That is why the f setting stops at 100 Hz. When measuring DC, set it to DC — otherwise power reads 82% high (the interface warns you about this).

Sample rate — what it means

Resolution is not the same as accuracy

What it meansOn this board
StepThe smallest digit it can read 1.04 mV
NoiseHow much the reading wanders with a steady input 0.039 mV
AccuracyHow close it is to the true value as good as the calibration reference (±0.8%)

The board can see a 0.04 mV change, but it can't say "this voltage is exactly 12.000 V" — to say that you need a reference more accurate than the board. In practice: excellent for comparisons and trends; for absolute calibration, as good as your multimeter.
